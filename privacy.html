Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Ledger Snap turns photos of receipts into a spreadsheet. It is designed so that your receipts never leave your device unless you export them yourself.

Receipt photos and extracted data

When you capture a receipt, Ledger Snap uses the camera and stores the photo in the app's private storage on your device. Text is recognised on your device with Apple's Vision framework, and the date, vendor, total, tax and category are extracted on your device, with Apple's on-device language model where available. The resulting rows, your notes and category rules are stored on your device. We do not upload, read, analyse or keep copies of any of this; Ledger Snap has no account system and no server.

If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup can include Ledger Snap data. That backup is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. If you delete a receipt, its row and photo are deleted from the app. If you delete the app, all of its data is deleted with it, except for files you exported yourself.

Photos you import

If you import receipt photos, you choose them in the system photo picker; Ledger Snap only receives the photos you select. If you save exported images to your library, the app asks for permission to add photos and never reads your library.

Exports and e-mail

Excel, CSV, PDF and ZIP exports are created on your device. They leave it only when you share them, for example with Send to accountant, which opens your Mail app with the files attached. The accountant name and e-mail address you enter are stored on your device only.

Purchases

Ledger Snap Pro is sold as an in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app, your purchase receipt from Apple, the app version, device model and country or region. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase and is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No receipt photos, amounts, vendors or notes
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts or location
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Children

Ledger Snap does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
