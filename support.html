Ledger Snap

Receipt to Excel, on your iPhone and iPad.

Point the camera at a receipt. Ledger Snap reads the date, vendor, total, VAT and currency on your device, picks a category and adds a row to your table. At month end, export a real .xlsx, CSV or PDF report and send it to your accountant.

  • On-device text recognition and AI extraction. No account, no server.
  • VAT column with rate detection (Turkish KDV %1 / %10 / %20 split by rate).
  • Totals that don't add up are flagged yellow, never guessed.
  • First 10 receipts free. Pro: one-time purchase or optional monthly plan.

Frequently asked questions

A total was read wrong. What do I do?

Tap the row: the receipt photo is shown next to the fields, and you can correct any value. Rows Ledger Snap is unsure about are yellow; tap Looks right after checking.

Which devices use AI extraction?

Devices with Apple Intelligence use Apple's on-device language model. Other devices use on-device text recognition with keyword rules for 7 languages; you can always edit the result.

How do I restore my purchase?

Settings → Restore Purchases, or the link on the purchase screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

In the App Store app: your account → Subscriptions → Ledger Snap → Cancel. The lifetime purchase is not a subscription.

Where is my data?

On your device, and in your iCloud Backup if you use it. Read the privacy policy.
